# Open Questions

Keep the interview conversational. These are prompts to return to naturally, not a form to fill out.

## ZeroNorth current chapter

- How would Morten describe ZeroNorth's next iteration in human terms, without NDA-sensitive details?
- Is the ambition to make complex maritime operations easier to reason about?
- Is it to turn the platform from a set of tools into something more assistant-like?
- Is it about moving users from searching/clicking toward asking, deciding, and acting?
- What are the hard constraints in this domain that make agentic AI difficult?
- What does "useful rather than theatrical" AI look like in this context?

## Engineering philosophy

- What makes a restriction good versus frustrating?
- Can Morten give examples of interfaces that are too open or too closed?
- How does this philosophy show up in frontend architecture?
- How does it show up in infrastructure tooling?
- How does it show up in AI tool design?

## Personal project graveyard

- Are there specific abandoned repos/projects that are especially illustrative?
- Which project has been restarted multiple times when new technology created an excuse?
- Which personal project became a daily driver because AI helped finish the last 20%?

## Collaboration and team shape

- Who are the kinds of people Morten works best with?
- What does he value in finishers/polishers?
- How has he learned to turn his exploratory energy into professional delivery?

## Career arc

- Which earlier professional roles best demonstrate the same pattern?
- Trendsales: did mobile/frontend architecture emerge from tinkering and boundary-crossing?
- Sampension: how did restriction/interface thinking influence cross-platform architecture?
- ZeroNorth vessel reporting/voyage optimisation: how did domain complexity shape product interfaces?

## Voice and boundaries

- How personal/funny should the public version be?
- Should the abandoned-repo/80-percent theme be explicit, or softened?
- How directly should the CV mention AI?
- What should not be said publicly?

## Domain example verification

- If using maritime workflow examples publicly, verify details or phrase them clearly as illustrative examples.
- Ask whether any specific examples are safe/accurate enough to include in the final bio.
- Clarify terms before final writing: master, bunker, hull cleaning, route optimization, underperformance signals.
