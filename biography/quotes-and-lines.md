# Quotes and Candidate Lines

Treat these as raw material. Some are direct user phrasing; some are draft formulations from the conversation and need approval before use.

## Direct or near-direct user ideas

> If I see an issue, I want to solve it.

> Engineering is the art of restriction.

> A programming language is the extreme of unrestricted.

> Product design is about removing everything not needed to allow the user to easily interact with what remains.

> Restricted enough to be usable, but not so restricted as not being able to solve the task.

## Draft formulations to consider

> I often build my own version of solved problems — not because I think the world needs another one, but because building is how I learn why the existing one looks the way it does.

> Building is how I read.

> I have a long history of rebuilding solved problems badly on purpose.

> My abandoned projects are not failures so much as field notes.

> The repo may die, but the concept becomes part of the toolbox.

> I do not collect technologies as résumé keywords. I metabolize them.

> AI has become the missing workshop assistant for the last 20 percent.

> The interesting question is not whether a system is powerful. It is where you put the walls so that the power becomes useful.

> Good interfaces are not about exposing everything. They are about removing just enough possibility that the remaining choices make sense.

> The interface can be a UI, an API, a platform contract, a prompt, a tool schema, or a deployment pipeline. The work is still deciding what to expose, what to hide, and what to make impossible.
