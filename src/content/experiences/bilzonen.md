I worked part-time on bilzonen.dk, an Umbraco-based .NET platform for the Danish car market. The job covered both daily maintenance and larger projects: a new search module, integrations with data catalogues, a mobile site, new-car catalogue work, and dealer-facing solutions.

One of my main contributions was a custom provider-model system in C# for moving data between external services, data stores, caches, and the site itself. I also helped establish the development environment, from virtual server setup to custom build and unit-testing tools.

It was early-career work in the best sense: close to the metal, broad in scope, and full of the kind of practical problems that teach you how systems actually behave once they leave the diagram.
