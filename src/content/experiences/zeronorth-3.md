I currently work on ZeroNorth's AI team, where the question is not "can we add a chatbot?" but "what happens when software can reason across the platform, use tools, and help people navigate genuinely complex operational decisions?"

The work combines AI engineering, product thinking, platform architecture, and a healthy respect for failure modes. Maritime operations are full of nuance: incomplete data, changing conditions, domain-specific language, and decisions where being confidently wrong is not charming. That makes it a good place to be careful, deliberate, and a little skeptical in the best possible way.

My focus is on building agentic systems that are useful rather than theatrical: connecting language models to tools and data, designing reliable execution patterns, shaping prompts and evaluation flows, and making the infrastructure boring enough that the interesting parts can be safely interesting.
