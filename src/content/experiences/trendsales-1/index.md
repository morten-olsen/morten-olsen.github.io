I started at Trendsales in a part-time role maintaining the API that powered the iOS app. It was supposed to be a fairly contained responsibility, which is usually how the wider rabbit hole introduces itself.

The API work quickly led into broader platform development. I spent a significant part of my time improving and supporting the mobile API, while the rest went into the marketplace platform around it: backend changes, integrations, and the everyday engineering needed to keep a busy consumer product moving.

It was my entry point into product engineering at scale — close enough to users to feel the impact, and close enough to the backend to understand where the bodies were buried.
