I joined ZeroNorth to work on vessel reporting: the unglamorous but critical bridge between what happens on board a ship and the decisions made on shore. The product was a Next.js-based, offline-first PWA built for crews who cannot assume stable internet, perfect timing, or calm seas just because the software would prefer it.

My work focused on developing and maintaining the reporting experience and the infrastructure around it. That included frontend application work, data-flow improvements, report-processing services, and Terraform-managed AWS infrastructure.

It was a good reminder that reliability is often a product feature. If software is used in difficult environments, graceful failure matters just as much as the happy path.
