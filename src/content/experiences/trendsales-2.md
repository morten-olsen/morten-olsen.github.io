I became responsible for the iOS platform at Trendsales at the exact moment where "responsible" meant "we need a new app, please make one." We built it from the ground up in Xamarin, alongside a new API designed to support the app and make the platform easier for others to extend later.

The first version went from project start in October to users' hands in late December. It was my first serious adventure in mobile development, and it quickly became a very real production system: more than 15 million screen views and 1.5 million sessions per month.

After the iOS launch, I joined two colleagues working on the Android version, and together we formed the mobile development team. I continued to work across the apps, the API, and the broader web platform when needed — a pattern that would become fairly typical for me: follow the product boundary until the technical boundary stops mattering.
